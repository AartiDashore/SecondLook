Second Look

A pause the member controls. Sound spots a risky moment, offers a short, plain-language second look before the money moves, and gets legitimate members through quickly. For authorized payments the member always decides. Only a likely account takeover can be stopped.

All data is synthetic (HuskyHack starter kit). Internal rule weights are illustrative design judgments, not fitted probabilities or fraud-rate claims. Dollar figures come from the Member Key and illustrate how each journey would change; they are not forecasts.
