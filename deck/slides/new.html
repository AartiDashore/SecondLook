Three ideas that go beyond a warning

Planned Moment

Members pre-announce a car, a closing wire or contractor payments. Lowers the score, never skips the check for a brand-new recipient.

My Pause Plan

A calm-day rule the member writes: wait 4 hours on any wire over $2,000. Voluntary, editable, deletable.

Trusted Contact

Told only that a payment was paused. No balances, no access, cannot release a hold. The member can choose to tell no one.

A relative or caregiver may be the exploiter, so the member directs every notice.
