Authorized scams leave members most exposed

Authorized scam

The member is tricked into sending money. Rules protect these payments least, and Sound can only delay and advise.

Account takeover

A criminal moves money without permission. Sound can stop the payment, but confirmations may reach the criminal.

Our rule

Advise when the member is the sender. Stop only when signals point to a takeover. Never decide by age or demographics.
